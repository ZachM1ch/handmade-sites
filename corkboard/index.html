    Tips & shortcuts
    • Drag items to move; drag empty cork to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom.
    • Drag from a pin to another item to tie a string. Tap a string to recolour or cut it.
    • Double-click a post-it or label to edit; a link or doc to open it.
    • Drop or paste images, files, and URLs straight onto the board.
    • [ / ] send backward / forward, add Shift for back / front.
    • Del delete · Ctrl+D duplicate · Ctrl+Z undo · Esc deselect
